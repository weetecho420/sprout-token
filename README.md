## Grower Nodes at a glance

- 1,000 licenses at 0.5 SOL each, max 5 per wallet
- Nodes earn 50 to 200 $SPROUT a day as they grow from Seed to Hero
- Rewards come from a pool of 15% of supply, paid over 2 years
- Creator rewards: 500 to 30,000 $SPROUT per X post, from a 5% creator fund

Rewards are estimates and not guaranteed. $SPROUT is a speculative token with no intrinsic value. Not financial advice.
