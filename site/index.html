Questions people ask

The short answers about Grower Nodes.

Do I need special hardware?

No. The Sprout Node app runs on a normal computer or phone. It just needs to stay switched on and connected to the internet to keep earning.

How do I pay?

With SOL from a Solana wallet such as Phantom or Solflare. Each license costs 0.5 SOL, and you can buy up to 5 per wallet.

What happens if my node goes offline?

It pauses. It stops earning and stops climbing stages until it's back online. You keep the stage it already reached.

Can I sell my license?

Yes. Your license is an NFT in your wallet, so you can sell or transfer it. Any unclaimed rewards go with the license to the new owner.

Are the rewards guaranteed?

No. The numbers on this page are estimates. Rewards depend on your node's uptime, they can change, and they end if the rewards pool runs out. The value of $SPROUT can also go up or down.

When does the sale open?

March 2027, about 6 weeks after $SPROUT launches on 27 January 2027. That way the token is live and the node app is fully tested before anyone buys. Follow Sprout on X for the exact date.
